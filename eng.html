Today, there are a large number of tubs on the market, made of different materials. Besides of composite, metal and wooden models are often found. Each of these materials has its own positive and negative sides.

Metal tubs are more durable and easy to clean, but they have their drawbacks. Such tubs have a swinging design and hang over an open fire, which poses a danger to their use. Metal tubs require a lot of fuel. To reach a comfortable water temperature, you first need to heat the vat for 5 hours, and then heat it continuously.

Wood is undoubtedly the most environmentally friendly material for tubs. But even here, the disadvantages outweigh the advantages. The main enemies of wood are fungi and mold, which make bathing in such tubs dangerous. In addition, if such tubs are not used for a long time, they will begin to dry out and crack. To prevent tubs from drying out, manufacturers often coat them with resins, which means the wood is no longer environmentally friendly.

POLARSPA tubs are made of durable composite material with a gelcoat coating from the German company BUFA. Composite tubs do not have the disadvantages of metal and wooden products, from appearance to easy maintenance and durability.
